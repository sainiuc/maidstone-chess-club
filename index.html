Welcome to Maidstone Chess Club

Do come and play at one of the most active and generally successful chess clubs in Kent. We have won every cup from the County Cup down to the Intro Cup!

Meeting Details

When: Wednesday evenings from 19:30 hrs

Where: St Faiths Centre, Moncktons Lane, Maidstone, ME14 2PY

Every week you can meet opponents for serious games and/or quick, casual games. Juniors are very welcome.

Recent Club Successes

  • 2013: Maidstone I won the County Cup (16th time) & Maidstone V won the Harvey Cup
  • 2018: Maidstone VIII won the Intro Cup
  • 2023: Maidstone IV won the Harvey Cup (6th time)
  • 2024: Maidstone V won the Tom Fuller Cup (5th time)
  • 2025: Maidstone IV won the En Passant Cup (3rd time)
