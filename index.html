About Our Club

Our recent successes include:

  • 2013: Maidstone I won the County Cup (for the sixteenth time) & Maidstone V won the Harvey Cup (for the fourth time)
  • 2018: Maidstone VIII won the Intro Cup (for the first time)
  • 2023: Maidstone IV won the Harvey Cup (for the sixth time)
  • 2024: Maidstone V won the Tom Fuller Cup (for the fifth time)
  • 2025: Maidstone IV won the En Passant Cup (for the third time)

We meet on Wednesday evenings from 19:30hrs at St Faiths Centre, Moncktons Lane, Maidstone, ME14 2PY. Every week you can meet opponents for serious games and/or quick, casual games.

There are club competitions, at both normal and rapid play, running throughout the year. You can join one of the teams (we have teams in 7 of the 8 Kent league divisions (County Cup, Lewis Cup, Stevenson Cup, En Passant Cup, Harvey Cup, Tom Fuller Cup & Intro Cup) and in national events, get advice on how to improve your game, or simply kibitz and chat! Juniors are very welcome.

For more information ring Bob Lane on 01795 842331 or David Heath on 01622 741758 or email David Heath at davidheath11@talktalk.net or simply come along on any Wednesday evening.
